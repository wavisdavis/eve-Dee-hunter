04 / Operational notes

  • The search covers only the regions where the selected pirate faction's sites spawn. Faction and empire selectors are linked, so impossible combinations cannot be chosen.
  • DED rating and acceleration-gate ship restrictions are separate from system security. A cruiser-class hull such as the Caracal Navy Issue can enter DED complexes rated up to 3/10; higher ratings need a battlecruiser (4/10) or larger. This finder locates candidate systems; it does not guarantee a particular DED complex will spawn.
  • Quiet index is a comparative score from the latest available activity snapshot, not a guarantee of safety or site availability.
  • Route length uses the ESI route service. Avoidance settings and autopilot safety settings in-game may differ.
  • For reliable results, run a fresh scan before undocking and verify system activity in-game.
